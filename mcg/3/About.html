About Us

Clinical Excellence Since 2004

Founder & Clinical Director

Mumtaz Chohan-Gani

BA (Sport Psychology) • BA Honours in Biokinetics • RAU

Mumtaz is a practicing biokineticist who has been practicing since 2004. She completed her undergraduate qualifications in 2004 at BA(Sport Psychology) at RAU and went on to complete her honours in Biokinetics, also at RAU.

She was previously a frequent contributor of exercise features to 'Your Baby' magazine and has been featured across various television and radio channels. She has presented at several international conferences and is a member of the Executive Committee and the Board of Directors for The Biokinetics Association of South Africa (BASA) as Marketing Director.

Mumtaz is deeply passionate about her work and seeks to make a difference in people's lives, demonstrating the incredible value biokineticists bring to healthcare. As an avid marathon runner, she understands the vital importance of living free of pain and performing at your highest level.
