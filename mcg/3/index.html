About Us

About MCG Biokineticist

Our practice prides itself in treating each patient as an individual. We ensure that a full and thorough assessment is done to determine the strengths, weaknesses and the cause of problem before we proceed with treatment.

We have a special interest in the treatment of back pain, sports injuries and orthopaedic problems. We treat special populations including pregnant women, kids and the elderly so that they may all train in a safe environment. We also see sports men and women in order to improve the strength and flexibility and help them achieve their goal. We have a holistic approach and refer our patients to physiotherapists, chiropractors, dieticians, speech therapists and other allied health professionals.

We have over our past 12 years of practice, received referrals from many doctors and other medical professionals.

  • Comprehensive one-on-one biometric evaluation
  • Evidence-based rehabilitative exercise programs
  • Safe, supportive, and private clinic environment
