Biokineticist

Mumtaz Chohan-Gani

BA(Sport Psychology)-RAU, BA Honours in Biokinetics-RAU

Mumtaz is a practicing biokineticist who has been practicing since 2004. She completed her undergraduate qualifications in 2004 at BA(Sport Psychology) at RAU and went on to complete her honours in Biokinetics, also at RAU. She was previously a frequent contributor an exercise feature to 'Your Baby' magazine and has been featured across various television and radio channels. She has presented at several international conferences and is now a member of the Executive Committee and the Board of Directors for The Biokinetics Association of South Africa (BASA), as a Marketing Director.

Mumtaz is deeply passionate about her work and seeks to make a difference in people's lives and demonstrate the incredible value all biokineticists bring to society. As an active person - Mumtaz is an avid runner - she understands the importance of the ability to live free of pain and to be able to perform at the highest levels, something she is deeply devoted to achieving with her clients.

The practice

About MCG Biokineticist

Our practice prides itself in treating each patient as an individual. We ensure that a full and thorough assessment is done to determine the strengths, weaknesses and the cause of problem before we proceed with treatment.

We have a special interest in the treatment of back pain, sports injuries and orthopaedic problems. We treat special populations including pregnant women, kids and the elderly so that they may all train in a safe environment. We also see sports men and women in order to improve the strength and flexibility and help them achieve their goal. We have a holistic approach and refer our patients to physiotherapists, chiropractors, dieticians, speech therapists and other allied health professionals.

We have over our past 12 years of practice, received referrals from many doctors and other medical professionals.
