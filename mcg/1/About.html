About Us

Clinical Excellence Since 2004

• Practice Founder •

Mumtaz Chohan-Gani

BA (Sport Psychology) • BA Honours in Biokinetics • RAU

Mumtaz is a practicing biokineticist who has been practicing since 2004. She completed her undergraduate qualifications in 2004 at BA(Sport Psychology) at RAU and went on to complete her honours in Biokinetics, also at RAU.

She was previously a frequent contributor of an exercise feature to 'Your Baby' magazine and has been featured across various television and radio channels. She has presented at several international conferences and is now a member of the Executive Committee and the Board of Directors for The Biokinetics Association of South Africa (BASA), as Marketing Director.

Mumtaz is deeply passionate about her work and seeks to make a difference in people's lives and demonstrate the incredible value all biokineticists bring to society. As an avid runner, she understands the importance of the ability to live free of pain and perform at the highest levels.
