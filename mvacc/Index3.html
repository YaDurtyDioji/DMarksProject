Our Services

Full-service accounting, tax and secretarial support

Every service is handled in-house, so nothing gets lost between departments — from monthly VAT to annual financial statements and company registrations.

VAT Calculations & Submissions

Accurate monthly or bi-monthly VAT201 calculations, reconciled against your records and submitted to SARS on time, every time.

Payroll

End-to-end payroll processing — payslips, PAYE, UIF and SDL — so your staff are paid correctly and on time, every month.

Payroll Reconciliations

Full reconciliation of payroll accounts and statutory submissions, catching discrepancies before they become SARS queries.

Annual Financial Statements

Compliant, well-presented annual financial statements prepared for companies, CCs and Trusts, ready for review or filing.

Independent Reviews

Independent review engagements carried out to the required standard, giving stakeholders confidence in your financials.

Income Tax Returns

Individual and corporate income tax returns prepared and submitted accurately, with attention to legitimate deductions.

Secretarial Services

Company registrations, CIPC annual returns and general company secretarial support handled from start to finish.

Trust Administration

Bookkeeping, tax compliance and reporting for Trusts, kept aligned with trustee and beneficiary obligations.

And Much More

Bookkeeping, management accounts, SARS dispute resolution and ad-hoc advisory — get in touch to discuss what you need.
